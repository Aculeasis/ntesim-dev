# Lacrimosa Nightmare damage and duration

**Responsibility:** Nightmare stack coefficient, duration/refresh, remaining-damage settlement and its MaxHP/healing dependencies, including local/published conflicts. Frozen cutoff: Global the Global source snapshot of 2026-10-04.

## Current evidence

- Local `Cau_LacrimosaBloodDam` initializes multiplier 1, clamp maximum 20, and additional coefficient `clamp(count - 1, 0, 20)`; positive expansion bypasses the added coefficient. The local model restores these defaults, but native executor and source attribution remain unbound.
- The local Nightmare GE serializes 3s A0 duration and conditional 6s duration, with `RemoveSingleStackAndRefreshDuration`. Native initial tick, refresh, overflow, and expiration order remain unresolved; the executable choices below are bounded hypotheses.
- A bounded executable hypothesis now maps `GA_Lacrimosa_Skill` and `GA_Lacrimosa_UltraSkill` to target-zero applications, five stacks per cast, a one-second `NeverReset` grid, and pre-scheduled `State.Damage.Dot` deliveries. The 16-second Rose test verifies fourteen off-field ticks, cap and thorn refresh. A separate 56-second continuation now checks one Skill, a 120-cost Burst reapplication, 31 ticks from 25s through 55s, and their full damage contribution to the DPS horizon. See [`lacrimosa-dot-scheduling-model.json`](../../../../reviews/q10-game-action-binding/lacrimosa-dot-scheduling-model.json), the [Rose runner test](../../../../crates/content/src/simulation/tests/rose.rs), and the [continuation receipt](../../../../reviews/q10-energy-ledger/lacrimosa-rose-continuing-energy.json).
- Local E/Q values 16/20 conflict with published 15/30. A separate public replay model tracks each application to its own effective-clock deadline, which conflicts with the local single-stack refresh rule. Preserve both until execution evidence resolves the semantics.
- The event enum correction identifies Nightmare's `BEING_TREATED` event; the enum name does not prove dispatch frequency or clock.
- The two reached Blood GEs explicitly serialize **StackLimitCount=10**. This differs from the coefficient calculator's **clamp maximum 20**; the current bounded scheduling model deliberately uses cap 20, as its historical receipt records. Neither field may silently replace the other. The source limit is available; native stack admission/overflow and cutoff applicability remain unresolved.

## Multi-target execution extension

The explicit `lacrimosa.nightmare_all_targets_v1` declaration extends the separately accepted scheduling model. It defers application to the admitted Skill/Burst impact timestamp, after same-time hit delivery, and applies only from a living source to then-living declared dummies. Skill's uncapped explosion also reaches those declared recipients. Each target retains its own stacks, refreshed aggregate deadline, one-second grid and pending deliveries; refreshing one train cannot cancel another target's ticks. The applying input captures Blood's **effective Normal talent**, including resonance, independently of Skill/Burst talents. Admission resolves coefficients and packet metadata before committing effects. The legacy declaration alone retains target-zero/cast-coordinate behavior.

The [native DoT tests](../../../../crates/content/src/simulation/tests/lacrimosa_dot.rs) independently check mixed talents, resonance, post-hit recipient selection and delayed reapplication. A first application at 0 followed by a second impact at 2.5s retains the original ticks at 1/2s and grid thereafter: each target receives `2*5 + 3*(10+9+...+1) = 175` stack-ticks, or exactly 36,050 noncritical damage across two targets at ATK 1000 and Normal 8. The [Rose tests](../../../../crates/content/src/simulation/tests/rose.rs) check all five refinements, shared 0.3s ICD across simultaneous recipients, post-hit grants, ten-stack cap, off-field ownership and exact expiry. One Burst gives forty stack-ticks per target, so T1 ATK 1140 contributes `2*40*.103*1140 = 9393.6` noncritical DoT; the last tick at 14s refreshes Thorn only until 17s.

The extension retains the selected cap-20/remove-one-and-refresh model, including its separate serialized cap-10 conflict. It does not resolve A1 native variant dispatch, A3 settlement, A5 healing or death after an already applied train. Native/WASM/Worker compare two continuing A0 Burst cycles after ordinary-hit energy refill. Public source retains its disclosed 100-energy research Burst inputs; existing direct owner scenarios separately exercise 120-energy casts.

Public Skill/Burst descriptors now attach the scheduling, all-target and fixed-critical-probability declarations automatically for DSL and Studio. An ordinary source rotation activates Nightmare without test-only request mutation or a required Rose weapon. Normal inputs alone attach no Nightmare activation. `lacrimosa_public.rs` independently checks a public unequipped Skill against two dummies at Normal8/Skill1: both first Blood ticks are515 at ATK1000, rather than300 from incorrectly using Normal1. The continuing Rose fixture and actual Worker fixtures now use the compiler's request unchanged. Historical raw requests retain their explicit scheduling/all-target opt-ins and their existing compatibility semantics; these defaults do not expand unsupported owner branches.
