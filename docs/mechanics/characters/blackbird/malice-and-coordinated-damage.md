### Marked-target bird controller

One controller **per Blackbird source**, with cast generation, marked-target expiry map, accepted-count0..5 and next-trigger deadline. At ordinary Skill admission clear the previous controller and all its marks, reset count0 and deadline to the cast time. Each of the4 connected Skill packets places/refreshes its hit targets' marks to hit+15s **without resetting** the cast's budget/ICD. This respects the native one source logic object, ClearAssistPotentialCount and OnCoopMechanismCleared; a per-target five-count alternative would multiply the explicitly bounded coordination when Skill hits several dummies. Field exit preserves controller/marks; owner death disables new triggers, scene/roster removal clears it.

Trigger only after positive applied HP damage by a **different currently active living ally** to a live marked target. Blackbird own hits, another off-field actor's passive, zero HP loss, mark application, Nova settlement and the bird itself do not trigger. At time>=deadline with count<5, accept once for the causal action packet (lowest target index among its marked recipients), increment count, set deadline=t+2, and deliver **one** SkillAssist AoE packet at that target immediately after the causal packet's HP/resource/Break settlement. Selected zero-travel dummy inference: the bird's1.5s cue lifetime is presentation, not evidence of a1.5s damage delay; immediate dispatch avoids inventing a travel duration. All live in-range dummies receive its full coefficient, not five birds or divided damage. A6 grants4 Malice once after that bird positively connects, only outside form; source charge/Cycle/Break once per bird regardless recipients. Spend a count on accepted coordination even if subsequent same-time target death prevents HP loss; no retry or count refund. Recast invalidates uncommitted old-generation requests. Already committed bird delivery uses captured source/generation and cannot rearm the cleared controller.

Example: marks on targets0,1,2; an ally packet damages all3 at t=1 → one bird/count1/deadline3, not three. Ally hits t=2 do not trigger; hits3,5,7,9 complete five, t=11 cannot produce a sixth even though the mark remains. A later Skill cast re-arms once; its second/third/fourth hits refresh marks but do not each allow five fresh birds. Same-time source callbacks use cast/packet deduplication; a2s boundary is inclusive. Replacing the active ally changes eligibility, not the source controller or its count.

### Stain and Nova enhancement

B2 is a team passive even off field. Apply the [shared Stain/Nova lifecycle](../../shared/cycles-and-break.md) first, with these owner overrides. **Stain:** base coefficient factor becomes **1.3** instead of1.2, its target duration **20s** instead of12, single stack/refresh. The native DamageUpRatio.10 adds to1.2; it is not1.2*1.1. The cue's8-second time-change input is interpreted as the extension12+8, applied once per generation; callback removal/reapplication is not repeated +8 on every tick. It still uses the actual Psyche/Lakshana damage actor's live Cycle Intensity, and does not copy Mental. Example at CI108 with E=1.075:1.3*1.075=1.3975, not1.419; for1000 eligible applied HP the extra397.5 is one nonrecursive copy.

**Nova:** one accumulator per target/active Nova generation. Snapshot Blackbird's **base ATK** on that generation's creation for the cap; accumulate actual positive **applied HP loss**, including eligible ordinary damage, Malice extras, birds and unrelated reaction HP loss while the generation is alive. Exclude the full causal Support/QTE cast that created that generation (including its children), every Nova Mental tick, and Blackbird's additional settlement itself. Resolve all recipients independently. Do not accumulate theoretical/pre-mitigation damage, overkill above remaining HP, shielding-only changes, healing or another target's damage. A generation created by the one automatic child has its own accumulator; the settlement that creates it is excluded and occurs before its window begins.

On normal expiry/dispel/refresh-end of an alive target's Nova, first resolve its shared pending Nova tick if that end calls for one, then settle `min(.20*accumulatedHP,1000*capturedBaseATK)` once. `GetPendingSettleDamageValue` returns this **already scaled/capped fixed value**; do not multiply it by.20 again. This extra is reported as Mental but is explicitly unaffected by other buffs/debuffs: no second crit, ATK, DEF, Mental RES, damage bonus, vulnerability, Stain copy or raw charge/Cycle/Break. It may reduce HP and end the target; no child or HP packet is manufactured after death. Example sum10,000,000/baseATK1000 yields1,000,000, independent of target Mental RES. At sum10,000 it yields2000; treating the helper as another ratio would wrongly reduce it to400. Source Source-snapshot SkillCoefficient stores fixed pending settlement, not an ATK multiplier.

After the initial eligible generation settles, reapply **one** fresh shared Nova at the same target, carrying `reapply_allowed=false`. That child still performs its own ordinary5s Nova tick,5.1s end and accumulator settlement, but never makes a grandchild. If a normal qualified switch refreshes a generation early, finish the old generation once with its current accumulator, then select the **authored switch's** new generation over a simultaneous automatic child; do not create two live Nova statuses or consume the child's guard to recursively refresh itself. Child generation end cannot reapply even after another callback. Owner passive removal cancels future Blackbird extra/reapplication listeners, not an already pending shared Nova. Target death/scene teardown cancels status/listeners with no postmortem damage. Aurelia's separate Nova-end listener observes each real end once; use deterministic party-slot callback order and the old generation ID so its follow-ups cannot be retroactively added to an already settled accumulator. Discord generation-pair admission stays in the shared reaction contract.

Consistency checks: a no-damage Nova can tick and create one child but has zero Blackbird settlement, not its own tick*20%; a generation with10,000 qualifying HP plus3000 triggering QTE and4000 Nova counts10,000 only; a natural initial+child lineage terminates after two real generations, whereas an unguarded end/reapply loop would never terminate. Native accumulator writer/filter/guard bodies remain unobserved; these explicit filters implement the independently described exclusions and bounded reapplication without disabling the passive.
