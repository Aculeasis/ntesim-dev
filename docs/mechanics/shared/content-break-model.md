# Content-runner target Break model

Responsibility: bounded target gauge and Broken-tag lifecycle for the content simulation runner. This model enables scenarios and content effects that need an explicit Broken target; it is not a native enemy profile or a complete native Break adapter.

## Bounded model

- A scenario may provide `GameTargetConfig.break_profile` with an exact positive `capacity`, an exact `current_gauge` in `[0, capacity]`, and a positive `broken_duration_us`.
- Public source admits this same explicit profile through `target break_gauge=... max_break_gauge=... broken_duration=...`; duration is seconds, exactly convertible to whole microseconds. All three fields are required together. Targets are anonymous; all stats and gauge inputs are explicit. Rust source tests check exact fractions, invalid/partial inputs and three continuing Break/recovery cycles. Native/WASM/Worker parity exercises explicit dummy profiles through aggregation and replay.
- Omitted `break_profile` means this target has no modeled Break gauge. There is no inferred capacity, duration, recovery rule, or fallback to the explicit-trigger reaction harness.
- Native packet metadata preserves `DT_SkillDamageData.UnbalValue` as an exact source scalar. Under the `combat.unbal_value_as_target_gauge_v1` hypothesis, a delivered hit with positive settled damage against a living target subtracts that packet's `UnbalValue` from the configured target gauge; a packet that defeats the target does not schedule gauge loss. A missing value contributes no modeled gauge loss; zero remains distinct from missing. This is a per-packet hypothesis, not a source-established unit conversion or complete gauge formula.
- Reaching zero while unbroken transitions the target to Broken and applies the content-owned `ntesim.status.broken` target tag for the configured duration. At expiry, the model removes the timed tag and restores gauge to configured capacity. No default timing or profile is inferred.
- A connected positive-damage hit by a configured Bohemian Rose wearer on a living, already Broken target may add the frozen extension duration to its deadline once per modeled Break generation. The tag expiry moves with the deadline; the old recovery event is ignored and a recovery is queued at the new deadline only when it falls within the scenario horizon. This behavior is isolated under [`weapons.rose_break_extension_v1`](../../../reviews/q1-rose/break-extension-model.json); it requires the explicit profile above and does not establish native profile, eligibility, or recovery behavior.
- Gauge loss is capped at zero. The runner does not apply the UnbalValue when the packet does not produce positive damage. Targets without a profile never transition through this model.
- Outgoing Break Efficiency scales that exact gauge loss at `ReactionOrBreak` settlement. Static `break_efficiency_multiplier` preserves source `UnbalAccrueEfficiencyBase`; old explicit scenarios default to one. Break Efficiency is not a public build stat: DSL and manual build additions reject it. Character-specific bonuses must come from their owning mechanics and apply only to the eligible packets. The runner adds eligible live `break_efficiency` buff bonuses through the shared recipient/stack/expiry/source-lifetime service, then multiplies once. Zero multiplier removes no gauge. The incoming gauge multiplier starts at one; no enemy pack is consulted. Ordinary damage, BI and Broken damage bonuses remain separate.

Daffodil's admitted Skill additionally transports its captured Resonance efficiency with each scheduled gauge event: additive1.25 per consumed stack at A0, replaced by2.50 at selected A1, at most two stacks. This owner addition sums with outgoing static/live efficiency before the selected incoming multiplier; it never scales true Break HP, Normal, QTE or Burst. The [owner capture model](../characters/daffodil/action-damage.md#executable-resonance-capture) owns Support admission, Echoes selection and independent continuing-cycle checks. Other owner-specific efficiency bindings remain separate.

The tag makes existing content effects that explicitly check `ntesim.status.broken` reachable through the same status service as other timed target tags. The new-generation transition separately schedules the HP contributions below; the tag alone adds no ordinary damage bonus or vulnerability.

Field presence does not establish units, packet eligibility, target gauge units, native efficiency, or whether a hit is delivered in every gameplay situation.

Rose owner tests additionally check the once-per-Break extension, synchronized tag/recovery deadlines, stale recovery invalidation, no-profile inertness, and strict hypothesis acceptance. These tests establish only the runner's selected behavior, not native-game behavior.

Independent runner checks establish `(1/3)*(3/2)=1/2` gauge loss and zero-multiplier inertness; live bonuses give `1/2-(1/6)*(1+1/2+1)=1/12` remaining gauge, versus `1/3` after expiry, recipient switch or death of a WhileAlive carrier. Snapshot buffs survive carrier death. Public-source checks reject manual Break Efficiency additions and run three continuing Break/recovery cycles with the native baseline. Native/WASM/actual Worker checks preserve compilation, reports, aggregation and replay.

The explicit reaction/Break research service has its own caller-provided shred and five-second fixture recovery in [reaction and Break harness](reaction-and-break-harness.md); those values are not defaults here. This model does not certify native enemy spawn/profile consumers or native efficiency consumer rules and does not implement dynamic enemy resistance/efficiency modifiers, remaining owner-specific Break bonuses/repeats/callbacks, Boss identity or native recovery semantics. Living-contributor eligibility and BI HP settlement use the explicit content hypothesis below. Rose's extension is a bounded runner rule, not a substitute for those native policies. Formula arithmetic remains owned by [damage and stat zones](damage-and-stat-zones.md#5-break-healing-and-shields).

## Live Break HP contributions

Decomposer's wearer callback runs only after an actual positive gauge decrease, before capturing a new generation's BI. Its wearer-only10-second BI buff and20-second ICD share the real settlement clock; partial/off-field reductions qualify, while semantic tags alone, absent bars, zero effective loss, already Broken/dead targets and zero starting gauge do not. The [owner model](../weapons/dustbin/passive.md#activation-and-trigger-mechanics) records the tooltip rationale, ordering limit and independent T1-T5 continuing checks.

`combat.live_break_damage_v1` binds each new gauge-to-Broken generation to one contribution per living deployed actor in slot order. The actor and all eligible source/target parameters are captured together after Broken admission. Pending packets retain that capture after a source switches or dies. A defeated recipient cancels the remaining group. Each contribution is a separate `ReactionOrBreak` event, counted by the normal event/work limits and report hit ledger; no readiness polling or additional clock is introduced.

The shared exact Rust formula is `base(level)*(1+BI/300+Break_bonus)*(original_capacity/3)*DEF*source_element_RES*special`. The consumed level1..80 `UnbaldamagePara` bases live directly in the content-owned [typed Rust array](../../../crates/content/src/simulation/break_damage/values.rs). Reports retain the exact `base_break` used by the formula; unused raw curve objects are not transported. Static `break_intensity` projects `UnbalIntensityBase` as points; live eligible `break_intensity` and `def_ignore` buffs use existing stack/expiry/recipient/living-source queries. Film - MANISH and Black Tome add their wearer BI once. A living Danzaburou carrier supplies its same-element aura when at least three deployed roster slots have that element, including the carrier; among eligible duplicate auras select the strongest once. Field presence does not gate the aura. This roster/living-carrier policy is a bounded model, not native consumer proof.

Ordinary ATK, crit and damage bonuses do not enter this formula. Each contribution rounds once at HP settlement, retains its individual owner/causal command/generation and appears in `hit_receipts[].break_contribution`, with zero ordinary scaling rate and explicit model packet provenance. Break packets consume no critical RNG, raw Ultimate/Cycle/gauge grants or ordinary action-hit proc callbacks. Fatal HP settlement dispatches shared target-defeat callbacks once. The accepted group is calculated before delivery, and failed/limited work remains unsuccessful rather than becoming an aggregate comparison candidate.

Daffodil's intrinsic Light Prism adds .20 in the Break-bonus zone, and individually selected A6 adds .15 DEF ignore. The [target Insight model](../characters/daffodil/action-damage.md#executable-target-insight-and-break-capture) additionally captures only this source's live mark count on this recipient: selected A4 adds .15 per stack to the same Break-bonus zone, and A5 emits one additional captured own contribution per stack. Baseline roster contributions retain slot order, followed by repeats in source-slot order; all are captured before delivery, and target defeat cancels remaining packets. [R6 Chaos reduction](../characters/daffodil/action-damage.md#executable-broken-linked-r6-resistance) is applied on the new Broken transition before this capture, retains its target deadline through Rose extension and clears on recovery/death. The causal ordinary packet keeps its pre-Broken resistance. Other owner-specific enhancements and designated Black Tome Arc callbacks remain in their owner queue rows.
