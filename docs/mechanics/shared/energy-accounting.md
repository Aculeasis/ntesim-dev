# Energy delivery and accounting model

Passive energy, stamina and custom-resource recovery use paused world time. During active Hotori Time Stop, ordinary Charge grants are suppressed before creating fractional carry or consuming a grant-dependent guard; suppressed grants are never deferred. Supported unrelated instantaneous direct-packet grants retain their own recipient and conservation rules. These are explicit shared timer-policy assumptions; they do not establish native timer bodies. See [clock composition](clocks-and-action-timing.md).


Responsibility: shared modeled recipient settlement, affordability and conservation. Native producer eligibility/amounts remain in character/weapon cards; this service does not turn an unbound source into generic generation.

An admitted action pays its typed costs once, before its packets/callbacks. Cost failure is atomic and cannot create a grant, cooldown or compensating refund. Ordinary per-packet sources use the owner's declared recipient split and delivery-time eligibility; delayed/off-field source ownership is retained. Exact Daffodil Branch/Entry, Aurelia AirAttack/EvadeBranch, Hathor Branch/AirAttack, Sakiri AirAttack and Chaos QTE direct inputs also reach this service under separate owner-scoped hypotheses; the shared adapter does not imply their natural dispatch or native energy eligibility. Chaos QTE is an exact explicit scenario packet pair, not a modeled QTE trigger.

For sources using efficiency/carry, exact grant fractions are scaled by each recipient's own Charge Efficiency and accumulated with that recipient's fractional carry. Whole units settle to its capped pool; the remaining fraction remains carry. Flat restoration and explicit fills retain their owner's separate policy and must not acquire an efficiency multiplier by analogy. Overflow is recorded separately from retained energy; full pools do not invent additional capacity.

```text
headroom = capacity - current
retained = min(gross_whole_units, headroom)
overflow = gross_whole_units - retained
initial + gross_grants = current + total_spent + total_overflow
```

Initial energy is not generated energy. Recipient, exact unscaled `charge_basis` for efficiency-scaled grants, packet basis where applicable, energy and fractional-carry before/after balances, source and gross/retained/overflow are recorded per event through the [runner ledger interface](../../../openspec/specs/combat-execution/reference.md#execution-and-checked-arithmetic). Ordinary-hit grants and the exact direct-packet inputs listed above carry `hit_basis`; Rust binds it to `charge_basis`. The event kind keeps direct-packet grants distinct from ordinary-hit observations even when both use the same owner source. Fixed direct amounts are checked from the [Zero](../characters/zero/action-damage.md), [Nanally](../characters/nanally/action-damage.md) and [Mint](../characters/mint/action-damage.md) owners; Mint resonance 6 also requires six selected awakening effects. Flat restorations omit `charge_basis` and preserve carry; supported source amounts are checked against the [Adler](../characters/adler/action-damage.md) and [Sakiri](../characters/sakiri/action-damage.md) owner policies. Zero carry may be omitted and means exactly `0/1`; admitted costs preserve carry. Native CLI/WASM/Worker observations must reconcile the same receipts, and Rust checks exact basis × recipient efficiency + prior carry against whole grant and resulting carry. Browser consumers check recorded continuity and final-state reconciliation without duplicating the owner settlement rules. Checked arithmetic rejects invalid capacities/deadlines/counters rather than wrapping or saturating a successful report.

This is the current tested settlement model; table scalar presence alone does not establish its source or recipient policies.
