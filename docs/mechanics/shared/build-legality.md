# Native build legality beyond preview constraints

Responsibility: whether a projected progression/equipment/substat selection is obtainable, rather than merely accepted by the static preview.

| Question | Checked boundary | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native random attribute pool membership, weights/tiers, main/sub exclusions, duplicate/reset/strengthening behavior | Frozen equipment rows name pools/slot counts; label/cost tables do not define the allocation rules | Supplied published values cannot certify a legal allocation or optimization domain |
| Minimum progression/acquisition prerequisites and stage-zero application | Base/growth/terminal pack arithmetic is available; selected cap is not proof of obtaining that stage at that level | Preview stats can describe an unobtainable selection |
| Meaning of `ExclusiveCharacterIDArray` and restrictions beyond the checked group match | Native group tokens are checked; exclusive arrays are preserved without imposing a guessed restriction | Full equip compatibility cannot be certified |

Known current interpretation, exact numeric values and supported constraints belong solely to [equipment builds](equipment-builds.md), [character progression](character-progression.md), [weapon progression](weapon-progression.md) and [console packing](console-packing.md). Console geometry and allocation are outside the model; console stats are authored explicitly.

Evidence: frozen `DT_Equipment`, `DT_EquipmnetCoreRandomAttributeData`, `DT_EquipmentStrengthData`, `DT_ForkItemData`, progression packs and original exports cited in those cards. Do not infer pool legality from published endpoints or impose the historical 15-line scenario on universal builds.

Return on applicable pool/roll/application and acquisition rules with independent legal/illegal examples. Until then preserve supplied-allocation hypothesis and unsupported optimization status; shared [release/applicability](evidence-applicability.md) remains separate.
