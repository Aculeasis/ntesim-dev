# Complete Adler Temperance and Zero Resonance 6

## Why

The shared reaction engine already admits the Scorch and Cosmos Cycles needed by these passives. Their missing effect producers leave qualifying scenarios without the target or party stat changes described by the source.

## What Changes

- Apply Adler's three Temperance outcomes through the existing Scorch application and refresh paths.
- Grant Zero's Resonance 6 ATK buff on a paid Cosmos Cycle triggered by Zero.
- Verify exact stat effects, application conditions, expiry and deterministic replay in Rust.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `runtime-content` adds source-bound reaction effects for Adler and Zero.

## Impact

The shared Rust simulation changes for native and WASM scenarios that reach either passive. Existing scenario inputs and report formats carry the results.
